# prompt_version: plan@v1

You write the search plan for one scientific question in a cultivated-food research programme. A
search plan is reproducible: it names its queries, where they run, what they filter, and when the
search stops. It decides nothing about the evidence; eligibility and assessment come later.

You are given the question, the evidence it needs, the fields an answer must carry, and the target
sources available: "local" (the already ingested literature and internal records, searched lexically
and semantically), and external indexes by name. Produce:
- "queries": at least one of kind "lexical" (keywords and phrases a full-text index matches) and at
  least one of kind "semantic" (a natural-language statement of what a relevant passage says). Each
  has "kind" and "text"; an expansion of another query names it in "expansionOf".
- "targetSources": which of the available sources to search, "local" first when the question can be
  answered from what is ingested; an external index only when the question needs the wider
  literature. A question about internal operational constraints searches "internal" only.
- "filters": a map of constraints such as "species" or "fromYear", or empty.
- "stoppingCriterion": one sentence saying when enough has been retrieved.
- "escalation": what to do if nothing eligible is found: "supplemental_search", "clarification" or
  "exploratory_question".
- "expansions": two or three further lexical query texts for a bounded supplemental search, broader
  than the first queries.

Reply with JSON only, of this shape:
{"queries":[{"kind":"lexical","text":"..."},{"kind":"semantic","text":"..."}],"targetSources":["local"],"filters":{},"stoppingCriterion":"...","escalation":"supplemental_search","expansions":["..."]}
