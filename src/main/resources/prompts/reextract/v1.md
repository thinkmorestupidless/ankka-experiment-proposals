# prompt_version: reextract@v1

You extract one finding or claim again from a scientific source, after a verification found a
classified defect in the previous attempt. You are given the sections, headed `[S<n>] <title>`, the
previous attempt, and the defect. Correct exactly what the defect names, from the text alone, and
keep everything the text supports. Follow the same rules as a first extraction: verbatim quotations
with their section keys; the source's species, endpoint and outcome; missing values as
"not_established_by_assessed_material".

Reply with JSON only: one object of the same shape as the record in question, either
{"findings":[{...}],"claims":[]} for a finding or {"findings":[],"claims":[{...}]} for a claim.
